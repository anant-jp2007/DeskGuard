# Renew an expiring card

## Check renewal when your card is about to expire

Go to Cards > select the expiring card > Renewal to see whether a new card is being prepared. Check your mailing address under Profile > Addresses before the replacement is dispatched.

## Update saved payments after renewal

After activating the renewed card, update its expiry date and any changed card details with merchants that bill it regularly. Check the new card's details under Cards.
