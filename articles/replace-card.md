# Replace a damaged card

## Request a replacement

If your physical card is damaged or unreadable, go to Cards > select the card > Replace card and choose Damaged. Check the delivery address before you confirm. The replacement is free and usually arrives within 5 to 7 business days.

## Activate the replacement

After the replacement arrives, go to Cards > select the new card > Activate card. The original card stops working when the replacement is activated.
