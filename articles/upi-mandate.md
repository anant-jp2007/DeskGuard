# Manage UPI AutoPay mandates

## Cancel a subscription mandate

Open Payments > UPI > AutoPay, choose the active mandate, and tap Revoke mandate. After the status shows Revoked, the mandate cannot be used for future debits.

## Find the next AutoPay debit date

Select the mandate under AutoPay to see the merchant, maximum debit amount, and next scheduled date. For a debit already submitted, open its entry in Activity to check its status.
