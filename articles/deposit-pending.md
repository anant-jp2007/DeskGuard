# Trace an incoming deposit

## Incoming bank transfer has not appeared

Check that the sending bank shows the transfer as Completed and that the sender used your current DeskGuard account number and IFSC. Allow up to 1 business day after completion for the deposit to appear in Activity.

## Report a missing incoming deposit

If the deposit is missing after 1 business day, go to Help > Incoming payment issue. Include the sending bank's reference, the amount, and the date of the deposit.
