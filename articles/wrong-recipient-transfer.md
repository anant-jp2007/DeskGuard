# Report a transfer sent to the wrong person

## Get help after sending money to the wrong recipient

Open Transfers > History, select the transfer, and choose Get help > Wrong recipient. Submit the transfer reference and explain which recipient details were entered incorrectly.

## Check whether the money can be returned

A completed transfer cannot be canceled in the app. DeskGuard Banking can review your report and explain the available recovery options. Recovery depends on the receiving bank and the circumstances of the transfer.
