# Set up UPI payments

## Create a UPI ID

Go to Payments > UPI > Set up UPI. Use the SIM registered to your DeskGuard account to verify your phone, select your account, and choose an available UPI ID.

## Find or share your UPI ID

Go to Payments > UPI > Manage UPI IDs. Tap Copy next to the active ID to share the address someone can use to pay you.
