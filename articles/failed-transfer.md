# Handle a failed transfer

## Money debited for a failed transfer

Open Transfers > History and check that the transfer shows Failed. If money was debited, DeskGuard Banking automatically returns the amount within 2 business days. If it has not returned after that, contact support from the transfer details and include the transfer reference.

## Try a failed transfer again

If the transfer shows Failed and no debit appears in Activity, check the recipient details before making a new transfer.
