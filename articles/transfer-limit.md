# Check domestic transfer limits

## Daily transfer cap

Domestic transfers are capped at ₹50,000 in total per calendar day. The limit resets at midnight India Standard Time. Go to Transfers > Limits to view the daily cap.

## If a transfer exceeds the cap

Wait for the limit to reset or lower the transfer amount to fit within your remaining daily allowance. The app displays the remaining allowance in Transfers > Limits.
