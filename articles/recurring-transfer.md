# Schedule an automatic recurring transfer

## Set up a weekly or monthly transfer

Go to Transfers > Scheduled > New schedule. Choose a saved recipient, enter the amount and start date, and select Weekly or Monthly under Repeat. Review the schedule before confirming.

## Stop future recurring payments

Go to Transfers > Scheduled, select the recurring series, and choose End schedule. This stops future occurrences. Select any payment already processing in Transfers > History to view its status.
