# Check domestic ATM fees

## Fee for cash withdrawals

The first 3 successful domestic ATM withdrawals in each calendar month are free. DeskGuard Banking charges ₹20 per successful withdrawal after that. An ATM operator can display its own separate fee before you confirm.

## Find your ATM withdrawal count

Go to Profile > Fees > ATM usage to see this month’s successful withdrawal count. Failed cash withdrawals do not count toward the free allowance.
