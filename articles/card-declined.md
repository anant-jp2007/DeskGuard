# Troubleshoot a declined card payment

## Check why a card payment was declined

Open Activity and select the declined purchase. Check whether the card is frozen, the transaction exceeds the spending limit, or the merchant category is restricted. Once you resolve the listed reason, try the payment again.

## Get help with repeated declines

If the card payment is declined again without a reason in Activity, go to Help > Card payment issue. Include the merchant name and the purchase date.
