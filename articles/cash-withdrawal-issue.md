# Resolve an ATM cash withdrawal issue

## Cash did not come out but the withdrawal posted

If an ATM debits a withdrawal and dispenses no cash, open Activity > select the ATM withdrawal > Report a problem. Note the ATM location and the date. Support sends an initial update within 2 business days.

## ATM dispensed less cash

Use the same ATM withdrawal report and include the amount of cash actually received. The app displays your case reference after submission.
