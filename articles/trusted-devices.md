# Manage trusted devices

## Sign out an old phone

Open Profile > Security > Devices, select the phone or tablet you no longer use, and choose Remove device. That device is signed out and must verify its identity before it can sign in again.

## Review recent device activity

The Devices screen shows the device type and when it last accessed DeskGuard. If you see a device you do not recognize, remove it and open Help > Account access issue.
