# Add a transfer recipient

## Save a new beneficiary

Go to Transfers > Recipients > Add recipient. Enter the beneficiary's name, bank account number, and IFSC. Review the details and confirm in the app. The saved payee appears in Recipients.

## Verify a saved recipient's details

Open Transfers > Recipients and select the beneficiary to check the saved name, account number ending, and IFSC. Compare these details with the recipient before sending money.
