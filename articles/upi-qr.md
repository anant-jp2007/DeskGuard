# Pay a merchant with a UPI QR code

## Scan a merchant's QR code

Choose Payments > Scan QR and point your camera at the merchant's code. Check the merchant name that appears, enter the amount if prompted, and approve the payment with your UPI PIN.

## Fix QR scanning problems

Allow camera access for DeskGuard in your phone settings. Hold the camera steady with the full code in frame. If scanning still fails, ask the merchant for their UPI ID and enter it through Payments > Pay to UPI ID.
