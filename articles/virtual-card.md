# Create and use a virtual card

## Find the virtual card for online purchases

Go to Cards > Virtual card > Create virtual card. When the card is ready, choose Show details and verify your identity to see the card number, expiry date, and security code.

## Check where you can use the virtual card

The virtual card works for online purchases that accept debit cards. Card details alone cannot be used to withdraw cash at an ATM. You can manage the virtual card's purchase controls from its Cards screen.
