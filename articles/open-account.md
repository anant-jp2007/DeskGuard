# Open a DeskGuard account

## Start a new account application

On the welcome screen, choose Open an account. Enter your legal name, date of birth, Indian mobile number, and email address. Verify your contact details and complete the identity check shown in the app.

## Resume an unfinished application

Choose Resume application on the welcome screen and verify the mobile number you used to start. The app returns you to the first unfinished step. Submit the existing application once you have completed every step.
