# Track a merchant refund

## When a merchant refund arrives

After the merchant issues a refund for a card purchase, allow 5 to 10 business days for it to appear in Activity. The merchant’s refund date starts that window.

## Follow up on a delayed refund

If a refund is missing after 10 business days, ask the merchant for the refund reference. Then go to Help > Refund issue with the reference and original purchase date.
