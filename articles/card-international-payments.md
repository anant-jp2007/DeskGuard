# Enable international card use

## Use your card abroad

Before traveling abroad, go to Cards > select the card > Controls > International use. Turn on the overseas payment types you need, such as in-store purchases, online purchases, or ATM withdrawals, and confirm.

## Check foreign currency charges

Go to Profile > Fees > International card use to see the foreign currency charges for your plan. For completed overseas purchases, Activity shows the billed amount in Indian rupees.
