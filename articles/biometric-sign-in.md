# Sign in with a fingerprint or face scan

## Enable biometric sign-in

Set up a fingerprint or face scan in your phone settings first. Then go to Profile > Security > Biometric sign-in in DeskGuard and confirm with your current sign-in method.

## Sign in if biometrics stop working

Choose Use password on the sign-in screen. After signing in, you can set up biometrics again under Profile > Security.
