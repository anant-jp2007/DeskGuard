# Freeze a lost or stolen card

## Freeze your missing card

Open DeskGuard Banking and go to Cards > select the missing card > Freeze card > Confirm. Freezing blocks new card purchases and cash withdrawals. If the card was stolen, report it to support from the Cards screen and request a replacement.

## If you find your card

If the card was just misplaced and you recognize all recent activity, go to Cards > select the card > Unfreeze card. A card marked stolen or already replaced cannot be reactivated.
